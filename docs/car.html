New for 2026 · 01 / 06

Active front wing

2movable flaps per side

In Straight mode the two upper flaps drop their trailing edges to shed drag, then snap back for the corner. On the Haas the actuator is thought to sit under the nose rather than in each wing half. Press Straight and watch them move.

New for 2026 · 02 / 06

Survival cell

768kg minimum, with driver

Tougher 2026 crash tests, a stronger roll hoop and a two-stage nose structure, in a car 30 kg lighter than last year.

New for 2026 · 03 / 06

Ferrari 067/6 power unit

350kW from the MGU-K

A 1.6-litre turbo V6 hybrid. The MGU-H is gone, and the electric motor delivers nearly three times last year’s power. Turn on X-ray to see it.

New for 2026 · 04 / 06

Smaller tyres

710mm rear tyre diameter

Still on 18-inch rims, but smaller all round: fronts are 705 mm tall and 280 mm wide, rears 710 mm tall and 375 mm wide. The model’s tyres are built to those sizes.

New for 2026 · 05 / 06

Flatter floor

−30% downforce vs 2025

The long ground-effect tunnels are out. A flatter floor and a larger diffuser make the car less sensitive to ride height.

New for 2026 · 06 / 06

Active rear wing

3elements, two of them movable

The two upper elements hinge open in Straight mode, lifting their leading edges so air passes through the gaps. It works in step with the front wing, on every designated straight, for every car.

Active aero

Corner mode

Closedflaps at full angle

The default. Every flap sits at its steep, high-downforce angle for braking and turning. The car returns here by itself the moment the driver brakes or lifts.

Active aero

Straight mode

Openfront and rear together

The driver opens both wings in a marked activation zone. At the front, the two upper flaps drop flat. At the rear, the two upper elements hinge open and gaps appear between them. Drag falls, so the battery’s energy carries the car further.

Power unit

1.6litre V6 turbo

A 90° V6 with a single turbocharger. Two banks of three cylinders, a carbon intake plenum between them, and exhaust primaries that sweep back into the turbine. Push the explode slider past halfway to pull it apart.

System 02 / 10

Hybrid system

350kW electric motor

The MGU-K sits low beside the engine and both drives the car and harvests energy under braking. It feeds a battery under the fuel cell through high-voltage cables, managed by the control electronics.

System 03 / 10

Fuel system

100% sustainable fuel

A flexible, puncture-resistant fuel cell sits between the driver’s seat and the engine, low and central to keep the weight balance steady as it empties.

Cooling

2sidepod radiator packs

Angled radiators in each sidepod cool the engine water and oil. The right-hand pod also carries the charge-air cooler that chills the turbo’s compressed air before it reaches the plenum.

01 · Active aero

Two cars in one.

DRS is gone. In 2026 every car changes shape on every straight: both wings open to shed drag, then close again for the corner.

High downforce

Corner mode

Flaps closed. Maximum downforce for braking and turning.

  • Drag100
  • Downforce100

Low drag

Straight mode

Flaps open. Drag falls sharply so the battery’s energy goes further.

  • Drag45
  • Downforce70

Bars are an illustrative index (corner mode = 100), not measured team data.

  • Front wing

    Two flaps drop

    The two upper flaps on each side rotate nose-up so their trailing edges fall flat. Analysis of the launch car suggests Haas drives them from one actuator under the nose.

  • Rear wing

    Two elements open

    The upper two of the three elements hinge so their leading edges lift, opening gaps that let air straight through.

  • Who decides

    The driver, then the car

    Straight mode is switched on by hand in marked zones. Braking or lifting puts the car back in corner mode by itself.

Haas has not published its flap angles or actuator layout. The motion in the 3D model follows the 2026 rules and published analysis, not team data.
