The machine · 2026

VF-26

The first Haas built for Formula 1’s biggest rule change in a generation: smaller, lighter, with wings that move and a power unit that makes half its power from electricity.

01 — Walkaround

Tap a point.
Learn the car.

Six things that are new for 2026 and where to find them on the car.

01 / 06

Active front wing

Flaps open on the straights to cut drag and close for the corners. No more DRS — every car has it, every lap.

02 / 06

Ferrari 067/6 power unit

1.6-litre turbo V6 hybrid. The MGU-H is gone; the electric motor now delivers up to 350 kW.

03 / 06

Survival cell

Tougher 2026 crash tests, a stronger roll hoop and a two-stage nose structure.

04 / 06

Active rear wing

Three elements that flatten in “straight mode” alongside the front wing.

05 / 06

Narrower Pirelli tyres

18-inch wheels stay, but fronts are 25 mm narrower and rears 30 mm narrower.

06 / 06

Flatter floor

Long ground-effect tunnels are out. A flatter floor and a bigger diffuser make the car less sensitive to ride height.
